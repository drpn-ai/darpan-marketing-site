One-time check on the ops-vs-finance framing bet after the 06-01 changes (added six ops subreddits + switched the miner to steady-state). **Verdict up front: the read is premature, and for a mechanical reason — no new data has come in since 06-01.** The framing question can't be answered yet because the experiment never actually ran.

## Are the new ops subs sampling? No.

This is the core problem. The six subreddits **are** wired into the miner config (`reddit_pain_miner.py` lists `supplychain, logistics, procurement, warehouse, freight, retail`), but the corpus contains **zero threads from any of them**. The corpus is still the original 12 finance/ecommerce subs:

```
Accounting 87 · smallbusiness 77 · Entrepreneur 60 · QuickBooks 55 · Bookkeeping 54
ecommerce 52 · shopify 50 · FPandA 27 · tax 23 · FulfillmentByAmazon 22
EtsySellers 17 · AmazonSeller 16
```

Corpus grew 530 → 540 (10 threads), but all 10 are in existing subs — none ops. The steady-state collector is not pulling new data. `window.out.log` shows why: the windowed run hit a **DNS resolution failure** (`Failed to resolve 'www.reddit.com'`) followed by repeated **HTTP 403** backoffs, and finished with `corpus=0 · +0 new`. This matches the known constraint that the miner only works on a machine with real network egress (it doesn't run in the sandbox). The 06-01 "switch to steady-state" therefore produced no collection, and the analyzer was never re-triggered on top of it.

## Honest verdict on the COO bet

The bet is **neither confirmed nor refuted** — the test hasn't executed. Don't read the frozen 2:1 finance skew as a result; it's a baseline drawn entirely from finance/ecommerce subs, so of course it leans finance. That said, there's a quiet early-warning sign worth holding: even within the original mixed subs, the clearly-framed pain runs 2:1 finance and the disagreeing-systems evidence clusters around bookkeeping stacks, not operations stacks. If the ops subs fill in and the ratio *still* doesn't move toward ops, that's the real risk to surface — reconciliation pain may be genuinely finance-*owned* in how practitioners express it, even if the COO is the buyer Darpan wants.

**Recommended next action:** this is an infrastructure fix, not a positioning decision. Run the miner from a machine with network egress (DNS + Reddit 403 backoff are blocking it), let the six ops subs accumulate a real sample, then re-run `analyze_daily.py` so `analysis_log.csv` gets a second row. Only after there's ops-sub data in the corpus is the framing read meaningful. Re-check then.
